OptiKPI

From an MVP to a reference point in its category

OptiKPI is a customer intelligence platform for gaming operators. It started as an MVP and grew for years in the hands of good engineers who had never worked on user experience. It is worth saying this the way it should be said: it was not a bad product. It had worked for years, it had real customers, and it had reached the limit of the structure it had been built on. Nearly every product that starts this way goes through that phase, usually at the worst possible moment, which is when the company begins selling to customers larger than the ones the product was designed for.

From there, every new feature cost more than the last. Complexity was being added to a structure that had never been meant to receive it, and the result was a product that kept speaking to the first kind of customer while the company was chasing another.

The decision, taken with the founder, was to redesign from scratch rather than correct. Putting the existing structure in order would have made a wrong structure more bearable, not different, and every month spent doing that was a month in which the problem came back larger. It is a decision that costs, and it was possible because the person who had to approve it accepted stopping before starting again. Version 2.0 was rebuilt from the information architecture up, with an AI agent inside the platform and a structure where the next features are added without breaking the ones already there.

Scope Full product redesign. Engagement from 2024 to 2026. Development excluded.
