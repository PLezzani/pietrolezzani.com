Edison, Risolve

From an offer that needed explaining to an ecosystem the customer assembles

Edison was bringing Risolve to market, a commercial offer that placed a set of home services alongside the energy supply. The problem was not how to make it look. The offer existed before there was a way to tell it. Presented as a list of services next to a bill, it asked the customer to work out what those things had to do with each other, and a customer who has to do that work usually does not do it.

The strategy we agreed on was to build an ecosystem with a centre and extensions rather than a catalogue. At the centre, the energy supply the customer already has and recognises. Around it, the services as extensions to be added according to what they need. That is the decision that ordered everything else, and it is the reason the work did not start from the interface.

We first put the existing flows back in order so they could carry that structure, and only then redesigned the customer experience, resting it on the design system I reviewed and extended so that site and app stopped speaking two different languages. The final phase carried those decisions intact to the people who would implement them, as requirements and as support to the agency in charge of development.
